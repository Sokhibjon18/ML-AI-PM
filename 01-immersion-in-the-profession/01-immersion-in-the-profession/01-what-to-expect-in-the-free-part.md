# What to Expect in the Free Part

> Module: Immersion in the Profession · Topic: Immersion in the Profession · Lesson 1
> Original: «Что вас ждёт в бесплатной части»

## Key ideas
- The free track of the "Launching and Managing an AI/ML Product" course is aimed at product managers / heads of product who want to apply AI in their work or move into the AI/ML domain. It takes roughly 30–60 minutes.
- Four lessons: (1) intro, (2) short course summary, (3) a real lesson from the paid part — a support-automation case, (4) wrap-up. Lessons can be taken in any order.
- The full course follows the whole AI/ML product lifecycle: choosing a problem where AI adds value → launch → measuring results.

## Course program (paid part)
- **ML product system design** — choosing data, model and metrics using an "AI Canvas".
- **Development management** — planning an AI feature launch and splitting work across the team.
- **Impact and economics** — calculating ROI, reading model quality metrics.
- **GenAI product system design** — designing a generative-AI solution and its unit economics.
- **Safety and risk** — ethical, reputational and legal risks of AI products.
- **Project cases** — realistic business scenarios (credit scoring, search ranking, support automation, etc.) that double as portfolio/interview artifacts.
- Detailed program PDF: https://code.s3.yandex.net/upskill/b2c_course_programs/AI_ML_product.pdf

## Learning format & support roles
- Interactive textbook, case-driven theory with mini-tasks, built-in AI assistant.
- **Curator** — logistics, deadlines, chats, motivation support.
- **Mentor** — answers questions in group chat, runs webinars, workshops, Q&A sessions.
- **Reviewer** — checks projects, explains mistakes and suggests improvements.
- **Peers** — cohort chat; a first professional network and potential future colleagues.
- The lesson ends with an optional intro survey (goals, reasons for taking the free part, current field).

## PM takeaways
- The course frames AI PM work as an end-to-end loop: problem selection, system design (data/model/metrics), delivery, economics, and risk.
