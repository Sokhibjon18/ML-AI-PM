# Course Overview Summary

> Module: Immersion in the Profession · Topic: Immersion in the Profession · Lesson 2
> Original: «Подытоживаем информацию о курсе»

## Key ideas
- Target audience: junior/middle PMs moving into AI/ML; PO/CPO/heads of product adding AI features; founders building AI startups; SMB owners automating processes with AI.
- It is a skills course, not an entry-level one: you should already know the product lifecycle, metrics, hypotheses and team work. No coding, model training or math required — ML and GenAI are taught at product level.

## Expected outcomes
- Spot problems where AI creates real business value, not hype.
- Pick the right tool (classic ML, LLM, RAG, fine-tuning) and estimate economics/ROI.
- Defend AI initiatives to team and stakeholders with clear artifacts and calculations.
