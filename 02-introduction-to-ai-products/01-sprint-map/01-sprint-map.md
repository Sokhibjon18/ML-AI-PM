# Sprint Map: Introduction to AI Products

> Module: Introduction to AI Products · Topic: Sprint Map · Lesson 1
> Original: «Карта спринта «Введение в ИИ-продукты»»

## Key ideas
- A one-week introductory sprint (most later sprints are two weeks). No project and no expert review yet — feedback comes from auto-checked problem sets with answer explanations. It levels everyone's baseline before the first project (AI Canvas, Module 2).
- Learning goals: explain what an AI product is and its components; distinguish it from a classic digital product; classify products by AI task type (NLP, computer vision, recommendations, search & ranking, forecasting); describe how AI PM work differs from classic PM work; judge with three criteria whether AI is worth applying to a task.

## Sprint structure
- **Topic 2 — What AI and AI products are.** The three core elements — **data, model, metrics** — and why they can't be considered separately. Compares a model-driven recommender with a rule-based price filter. Ends with a problem set.
- **Topic 3 — AI product types by task.** A map of task types; for each: input data, output, typical products/industries. Three cross-industry examples and a self-study task to find AI components in familiar apps. Why it matters: identifying task type first determines data needs, metrics, quality checks and alternatives.
- **Topic 4 — Role of the PM in AI products.** Data as part of product requirements, framing ML tasks, experiments, model quality evaluation; classic PM vs AI PM comparison; a career-switch story. Problem set: tell ordinary product situations from AI-specific ones, and assess AI suitability with three criteria. Optional reading list (articles, market research, job postings).

## How to study
- Go linearly; do each short problem set right after the material. Mistakes are part of learning.
- Help: platform search, built-in AI assistant, mentor chat.
- Assumes existing PM fundamentals; focuses only on what's specific to AI products. Starts with an activation quiz.
