# Sprint Map

> Module: Managing AI Product Development · Topic: Sprint Map · Lesson 1
> Original: «Карта спринта»

## Key ideas
- After producing a validated concept (AI Canvas + explanatory note), the next question is how to turn it into a working product: who owns what, how to organize work, and when a model is needed vs a simple rule.
- This sprint (the course's third) is about managing AI product development. The lesson includes an intro video (not captured in this note).

## Frameworks / methods
Sprint structure — one step per topic:
1. Development lifecycle.
2. Team.
3. Work organization.
4. Trade-offs between heuristics and learning (rule vs model).
5. Extended case "Puls" (telemedicine): the same planning logic under harder conditions — one task that is really two, and data for the future model not yet ready.

Final deliverable: a **Kanban board** with tasks distributed across roles (Data Engineer, Data Scientist, ML Engineer), covering the path from first prototype to full launch, the chosen way of organizing the team, and a justification of where a model vs a rule is used.

## Case / example
Project case: **"Stupen"**, an online vocational school for adults — same story as the previous sprint (slow project-work reviews, growing review queue, churn of students who didn't get feedback in time). Now the task is to plan implementation (team, roles, stages), not design the concept.

Assessment: no expert review — self-assessment against a detailed checklist, then peer review with another student (be stricter with yourself; reviewing a peer builds perspective).

## PM takeaways
- Follow the linear route; do problem sets right after each topic — they train the exact action needed for the project.
- Helpers: platform search for terms, AI assistant, mentor chat.
